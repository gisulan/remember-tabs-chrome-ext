# Changelog

All notable changes to Remember Tabs. Each version below is a stable release you can download from
[Releases](https://github.com/gisulan/remember-tabs-chrome-ext/releases).

Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`:
- **patch** (1.0.**1**): bug fixes only;
- **minor** (1.**1**.0): new features, and your data is kept;
- **major** (**2**.0.0): big changes that may need you to do something.

## v1.0.0 (2026-11-26)

First stable release. Works in **Microsoft Edge, Brave and Google Chrome** (version 116 or newer) on **Windows and macOS**.

### Features
- **Last session:** your windows, tabs and groups are saved continuously. After the browser or computer restarts, reopen everything, one window, one group or single tabs. Nothing reopens on its own. The last 10 sessions are kept.
- **Library of saved groups:** save an open group, choose whether to close its tabs to free memory, and reopen it later with its name and color. Saved groups stay in the library after you reopen them.
- **Add pages to saved groups without opening them**, from any open tab or the current page.
- **Lazy reopening:** restored tabs stay asleep until you click them. A toggle at the bottom of the panel switches this off.
- **Search** across open tabs, the library and the last session at once.
- **Duplicate warnings:** you're warned when a page (exact URL) is already saved in any group. Duplicates are also labeled in the library and among open tabs.
- **Drag-and-drop** sorting of groups, and of pages within and between groups.
- **OneDrive backup** to your own OneDrive (personal Microsoft accounts; work accounts if your company allows it). It backs up automatically, shows its status at the bottom of the panel, and offers **Restore…** to bring your groups into another browser or computer.
- **Export / Import** of everything to a JSON file.
