# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-11-26)

First release, for Edge, Brave and Chrome (version 116 or newer).

- **Last session:** your windows, tabs and groups are saved continuously. After a restart you can reopen everything, one window, one group or single tabs. The last 10 sessions are kept.
- **Library of saved groups:** save an open group, optionally close it to free memory, and reopen it later. You can add pages to saved groups without opening them.
- **Lazy reopening:** restored tabs stay asleep until clicked. This can be switched off.
- **Search** across open tabs, the library and the last session.
- **Duplicate warnings** for exact-URL matches across all saved groups.
- **Drag-and-drop** sorting of groups and pages.
- **OneDrive backup** to your own OneDrive, with Restore and a visible status line. Export/Import to a JSON file is also available.

**Install:** download `remember-tabs-v1.0.0.zip` below, unzip it to a permanent folder, enable Developer mode on your browser's extensions page, then click **Load unpacked** and select the folder. Full steps are in the [README](README.md#install).
